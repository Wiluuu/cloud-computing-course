## Comparison Analysis Questions

1. **Which cloud provider offers the broadest range of services?**  
   AWS offers the broadest range of services with the deepest catalog of infrastructure features, market maturity, and ecosystem integrations available in public cloud computing today.

2. **Which provider best integrates with Microsoft technologies?**  
   Microsoft Azure integrates best with Microsoft technologies, providing native support, licensing advantages, and effortless synchronization with Active Directory, Windows Server, and Office 365 environments.

3. **Which provider is strongest in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes?**  
   Google Cloud Platform (GCP) is the strongest provider for AI and Kubernetes, capitalizing on Google's creation of Kubernetes and deep expertise in advanced machine learning platforms like Vertex AI and BigQuery.

4. **Which cloud platform would you personally choose and why?**  
   I would personally choose AWS due to its massive documentation catalog, industry dominance, wide job market demand, and well-rounded service ecosystem that makes building scalable web and mobile applications straightforward.
